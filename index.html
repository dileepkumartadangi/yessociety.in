Self-reliant communities start with opportunity

YESS is a registered non-profit working in Parvathipuram Manyam District, Andhra Pradesh. We work closely with tribal and rural communities to improve access to education, build skills and create sustainable livelihoods.

Through community-driven programs, we help youth and women become leaders of change — building leadership, social inclusion and the capacity for long-term development.

Our vision

An inclusive society where empowered youth and women lead sustainable social and economic development.

Our mission

To uplift marginalized communities through education, entrepreneurship, leadership development and life skills.

What we work on

Six areas where we focus our time, people and resources.

Education

Access to quality education and learning opportunities.

Youth development

Leadership, skills and career guidance for young people.

Health & sanitation

Hygiene awareness and access to better healthcare.

Women empowerment

Education, leadership and livelihoods for women.

Charity & welfare

Welfare programs that support vulnerable communities.

Environmental protection

Sustainability and environmental awareness initiatives.
